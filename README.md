# Two Lines to Your Past Self

A make.fun entry for [Two Lines to Your Past Self](https://make.fun/bounty/u-suga-two-lines-to-your-past-self-f36c83ca): two lines sent back to the writer's past self.

Line one warns about one specific moment — saying yes to an overtime shift on Christmas Eve just to prove he could handle it. Line two traces what that moment cost: his daughter's first Christmas morning, and every Christmas since spent trying to make up for a morning that never comes back. One sentence per line, one moment, one consequence.

## The entry

> Don't say yes to the overtime shift on Christmas Eve just to prove you can handle it.
> That one yes meant you missed your daughter's first Christmas morning, and you have spent every Christmas since trying to make up for a morning that never comes back.

## How it runs

`main.py` writes the two lines to `/output/entry.txt` (252 characters, two lines, one sentence each).
